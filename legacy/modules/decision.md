# Domain adapter — decision without execution

Invoked from the router at START when the deliverable is a judgment and no
tools are needed. Elaborates K1–K6; creates no new prohibition.

## Stakes

`imp` and `due` are frozen with the envelope under K1. At `imp` ≥ 4, name at
least two approaches and why the chosen one wins, recorded before the first
production step. At `imp` ≥ 4 the bar on `r` also rises: `advanced` requires an
independent or held-out check and every load-bearing fact verified at its
source rather than recalled (`reference/matrix.md` 8c). Urgency from `due` may
reduce ceremony above that bar, never below it.

## What evidences `r` here

- Every hard constraint evaluated against **every** option, with the
  arithmetic or derivation shown rather than asserted.
- Every rejected option eliminated on a cited constraint, before any
  quality comparison — never eliminated on impression. If no option is
  infeasible, say so; a manufactured elimination is worse than none.
- Exactly one commitment, stated plainly, with the condition that would
  reopen it.
- Unknowns named, not resolved by invention: what is unknown, why it
  matters, what would resolve it. A missing fact is never replaced by a
  plausible one, and confidence is never manufactured to close a gap.

`r > 0` means an option was eliminated on evidence, a constraint was checked
against the facts, or the commitment was fixed with its reopening condition —
not that more prose was written. `r < 0` means a committed decision was
silently reversed, or an option eliminated on a constraint was quietly
readmitted.

## The loop, per unit of work

1. Pick the constraint or option whose resolution most changes the answer.
2. In the process channel, state `expect[r]: ...` before acting, and
   `expect[i]: ...` only when material.
3. Do the check inside the envelope.
4. Verify against the stated facts; classify `r` and `i` separately.
5. Compare each prediction with its own target; with no prior `expect`,
   record `δ: unscorable`.
6. On no verified progress or repeated information-only outcomes, go to
   `stall-or-replan.md`.

## Exits

- Outside-envelope alternative recognized, including one the user implied →
  `proposal.md`.
- Before the final response → `completion.md`, unconditionally.
